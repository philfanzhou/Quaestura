# Question Catalog

The Question Catalog defines reusable learning questions and the language used to classify them. Learner-specific mistake records belong to Mistake Learning.

## Language

**Question**:
A question definition that can be reused across multiple learning scenarios.
_Avoid_: Mistake Item, Homework

**Question Content**:
The stem, answer, explanation, and other content of a Question.
_Avoid_: Document File, Submission

**Knowledge Point**:
A hierarchical concept, organized by Subject and Grade, that describes the knowledge a Question assesses.
_Avoid_: Knowledge, Tag

**Tag**:
A free-form label maintained by staff to supplement Question search and classification.
_Avoid_: Knowledge Point, Root Cause
